ABOUT THE PROJECT

Smarter campus maintenance.

CampusFix AI is a hackathon prototype for making campus complaint reporting and follow-up more organized.

⌁

Smart triage

Rule-based logic suggests a category and priority from the complaint description. Staff can review and override suggestions.

◎

Duplicate hints

Text similarity and matching locations help flag reports that may describe the same issue. Nothing is automatically deleted.

↗

Transparent tracking

Complaint status, staff assignment, and activity notes help demonstrate how issues move toward resolution.

Prototype limitations

This version does not include real user authentication, email notifications, a production AI model, or deployment hardening. The complaint management controls are intended for a local demo. Add secure authentication and authorization before using it with real student data.
